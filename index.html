About Me

I am a computer scientist, molecular pharmacologist, physiologist, and biochemist. Currently finishing computer science MS student in my last year. I am a fast learner and motivated team player. My PhD studies focused on the sequelae of supplemental oxygen in premature lungs, concluding it causes senescence and metabolic dysregulation. Postdoctoral fellowship on tumor microenvironment and senescence at Merck. VC experience from a 9-month rotation at MRL Ventures Fund. Currently looking for a co-op starting in January 2026.
